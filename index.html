Human Resources
Talent Resources.

Talent is no longer only human. The function that builds the workforce must now source, qualify, deploy and retire both human and AI talent, and balance them for a net-positive outcome.

Human talent

People who bring judgment, accountability, ethics, relationships and context that no model owns.

AI talent

Digital agents and physical (embodied) systems that bring tireless execution, breadth and speed, with skills that can be acquired, versioned and swapped.

The TR team

The function that reads the market, the business need and both talent pools, then composes the team that wins.

Three shifts

Today (HR)Tomorrow (TR)
Headcount is the unit of capacity.Capability-hours across humans and AI are the unit of capacity.
A human lead manages the team.Where it is safer and better, an AI lead coordinates the work and coaches the humans in it.
Hire, onboard, review, exit people.Source, qualify, deploy, evaluate and retire any talent, with the same rigor and an audit trail.
Humans do the work; compliance reviews later.Humans own the ethical, safety and responsible-AI checks, built into the team's design from day one.

The idea in one picture

Market signals→Business needs→Human + AI talent pools→TR balancing→Hybrid pods→Net-positive outcome

The hard problem is not "how many AI agents can we buy." It is matching: which work should be led by AI, which must stay human-led, how to keep people growing rather than displaced, and how to prove the whole arrangement is a net gain for customers, the business and the people in it.
