Interoperability

Humans, digital AI and physical AI, one workforce

Interoperable means any kind of talent can be assigned, supervised, evaluated and replaced through the same system.

Human

Judgment, ethics, relationships, accountability.

Digital AI

Agents and models doing knowledge work: planning, analysis, design, code, operations.

Physical AI

Embodied systems: robots, vehicles, devices doing work in the physical world.

The Talent Passport

One record format for every worker, human or AI, so TR can compare and compose them.

Shared protocols

  • Work assignment: tasks are routed by skill and authority, not by whether the worker is a person.
  • Handoffs: structured context transfer between humans and AI in either direction.
  • Escalation: every AI role has a defined path to a human decision-maker.
  • Audit: who (or what) did what, on whose authority, is always reconstructable.

Physical AI adds

  • Physical safety cases and on-site human safety supervisors
  • Maintenance and downtime as part of "availability"
  • Insurance, liability and site-access rules in the Talent Passport
